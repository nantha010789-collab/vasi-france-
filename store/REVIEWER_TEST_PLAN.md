# VASI reviewer test plan

## Customer
1. Sign in with the reviewer customer account.
2. Allow location while using the app.
3. Select pickup and destination.
4. Review fare and vehicle type.
5. Create a ride request in the reviewer/test environment.
6. Open Safety Centre and verify trip sharing/account deletion links.
7. Open Eats and view a restaurant/order flow.

## Driver
1. Sign in with an approved reviewer driver account.
2. Verify documents show approved.
3. Go online.
4. Receive a test ride offer.
5. Accept, navigate, verify PIN, start and complete the ride.
6. Review earnings and payout status.

## Restaurant
1. Sign in with an approved reviewer restaurant account.
2. Open/close the restaurant.
3. View/edit menu items.
4. Receive and process a test order.
5. Review payout status.

## Important reviewer notes
- AI is advisory only and never approves drivers, restaurants, refunds, payments or account deletion.
- Safety, payment, refund and fraud cases require human review.
- Emergency assistance in France/EU directs users to 112.
